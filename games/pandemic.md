# Pandemic

Cooperative — 2–4 players. Cure all four diseases before the team loses.

## **Setup**

---

### Board setup

- Place board.
- Sort disease cubes by color (blue, yellow, black, red).
- Put 1 research station in **Atlanta**.
- **Markers:**
  - Outbreak marker on **0**.
  - Four cure markers (vial side up) near cure track.
  - Infection rate marker on the first **2** space.
- **Initial infection:**
  - Shuffle Infection deck.
  - Draw 3 cities → place **3 cubes** on each.
  - Draw 3 more → place **2 cubes** on each.
  - Draw 3 more → place **1 cube** on each (18 cubes total).
  - Discard those 9 cards to the Infection discard pile.
- **Player deck:**
  - Choose difficulty — Introductory **4** / Standard **5** / Heroic **6** Epidemic cards.
  - Split Player deck into that many equal piles.
  - Shuffle 1 Epidemic into each pile.
  - Stack piles to form the draw deck.

### Player setup

- Each player takes a role card, pawn, and reference card.
- Place all pawns in **Atlanta**.
- **Deal starting Player cards:**
  - **2 players:** 4 each
  - **3 players:** 3 each
  - **4 players:** 2 each

### Choosing starting player

Player holding the **highest-population city** card in their starting hand goes first.

## **Play**

---

### Step-by-step turn

1. Take up to **4 actions** (any order, any combination).
2. Draw **2 Player cards**.
3. **Infect** — flip Infection cards equal to the current infection rate.

Play passes left. Discuss freely; the active player makes final decisions.

### Commonly overlooked rules

- No replacement draw after drawing an Epidemic.
- On **your** turn, you may **take** a city card from another player if you are both in that card's city.
- On **your** turn, you may take **any** city card from the **Researcher** if you share a city.
- Hand limit applies **immediately** after receiving a card from another player.

### Turn step details

#### Actions (4 per turn)

**Movement** (each costs 1 action):

- **Drive/Ferry** — move to an adjacent city (white line).
- **Direct Flight** — discard a city card → move to that city.
- **Charter Flight** — discard the card matching your **current** city → move anywhere.
- **Shuttle Flight** — move between two cities that both have research stations.

**Other** (each costs 1 action unless noted):

- **Build Research Station** — discard your current city's card → place a station there.
- **Treat Disease** — remove **1** cube from your city (**all** cubes of that color if the disease is cured).
- **Share Knowledge** — give or take the city card matching your current city with another player in the same city (both must agree). See Commonly overlooked rules above.
- **Discover a Cure** — at a research station, discard **5** cards of one color (**4** for Scientist) → place that disease's cure marker.

#### Draw 2 Player cards

- Resolve **Epidemic** cards immediately, then continue drawing if you have draws remaining.
- Do **not** draw a replacement card after an Epidemic.
- Enforce **hand limit 7** — discard immediately if over limit (including right after receiving a card from another player).

**Epidemic** — resolve in order:

- **Increase** — advance infection rate marker one space.
- **Infect** — draw bottom Infection card; place **3** cubes on that city (skip if eradicated).
- **Intensify** — shuffle Infection discard pile; place it on top of the Infection deck.

#### Infection phase

- Flip cards equal to current infection rate.
- For each card, add **1** cube of that color to the city (skip if eradicated).
- City already has **3** cubes of that color → **outbreak** instead of placing a 4th.

## **Additional details**

---

### Outbreaks

- Advance Outbreak marker **1** space.
- Place **1** cube of that color in **every connected city** (skip eradicated diseases).
- If a connected city already has **3** cubes of that color → **chain outbreak** (marker advances again).
- **8 outbreaks** → team loses.

### Cured vs eradicated

- **Cured** — Treat Disease removes all cubes of that color; Discover a Cure at **any** research station (station's city color need not match the disease).
- **Eradicated** — all cubes of that color removed from the board; that disease can no longer infect or outbreak.

### Win and lose

**Win** — cures discovered for all four diseases (immediate).

**Lose** — any of:

- Outbreak marker reaches **8**
- Cannot place cubes (supply of any color exhausted)
- Cannot draw **2** Player cards

### Role abilities (quick reference)

| Role | Ability |
|------|---------|
| **Dispatcher** | As a move action, move any pawn to a city that contains another pawn. |
| **Medic** | Treat Disease removes **all** cubes of one color (not just 1). |
| **Scientist** | Discover a Cure with **4** cards of one color (not 5). |
| **Researcher** | Give any card when sharing; others may take **any** city card from you if you share a city. |
| **Operations Expert** | Build a research station **without** discarding a city card. |
| **Contingency Planner** | As an action, take an Event card from the discard pile onto your role card; play it later from there. |
| **Quarantine Specialist** | While you are in a city, prevent disease cubes from being placed in that city and all connected cities. |

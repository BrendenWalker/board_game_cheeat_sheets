# Pandemic: Reign of Cthulhu

Cooperative — 2–4 players. Seal all **4** gates before the Old Ones win.

## **Setup**

---

### Board setup

- Place board; put **Cthulhu** Old One card **facedown** on its space.
- Set **cultist** and **Shoggoth** figures and **Seal** tokens nearby.
- **Old Ones:**
  - Shuffle Old One cards; place **6** facedown to the left of Cthulhu.
  - Return unused Old One cards to the box.
- **Summoning deck:**
  - Shuffle Summoning cards.
  - Flip **2** → place **3** cultists on each location.
  - Flip **2** more → place **2** cultists on each location.
  - Flip **2** more → place **1** cultist on each location.
  - Flip **1** more → place **1** Shoggoth on that location.
  - Place all **9** drawn cards faceup on the Summoning discard pile.
- **Player deck:**
  - **Clue difficulty:**
    - Introductory — use all **44** Clue cards.
    - Standard — remove **1** Clue of each color from the deck.
    - Expert — remove **2** Clues of each color from the deck.
  - Select **4** / **5** / **6** Relic cards (2 / 3 / 4 players); shuffle them with the remaining Clue cards to form the Player deck.
  - Place unused Relic cards facedown as the **Relic pile**.
  - Divide the Player deck into **4** facedown piles (as equal as possible).
  - Shuffle **1** Evil Stirs card into each pile.
  - Stack piles to form the draw deck (smaller piles on the bottom).

### Player setup

- **Draft investigators:**
  - Shuffle the **7** Investigator cards; deal **2** faceup.
  - First player picks one; replace it from the deck.
  - Next player picks from the **2** showing; continue until each player has an Investigator.
  - Return unused Investigators and figures to the box.
- Each player places their figure at the **Train Station** in **Arkham**.
- Each player takes **4** sanity tokens and keeps their Investigator card **sane-side up**.
- Each player takes a **Reference** card.
- **Deal starting hands** (faceup suggested):

| Players | Cards |
|---------|-------|
| 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 2 |

### Choosing starting player

Player who most recently read a horror story goes first.

## **Play**

---

### Step-by-step turn

1. Take up to **4 actions** (sane side of Investigator card; role abilities may change this).
2. Draw **2 Player cards** (resolve Evil Stirs).
3. **Summoning** — reveal cards equal to the current summoning rate.

Play passes left. Discuss freely; the active player makes final decisions.

### Commonly overlooked rules

- Relic cards and Clue cards are different, but both count as Player cards.
- No replacement draw after drawing an **Evil Stirs** card.
- On **your** turn, you may give or take a card from another player in the same location (any **Relic**, or a **Clue** matching your current town); both must agree.
- **Hand limit 7** applies at all times (including immediately after receiving a card).
- Roll the **Sanity die** when you **enter** a Shoggoth’s location, or when a Shoggoth **enters** your location (placed or moved).

### Turn step details

#### Actions (4 per turn unless noted)

**Movement** (roll Sanity die if movement enters a location with a Shoggoth):

- **Walk** — move to an adjacent location (connected by a line).
- **Take the Bus** (must be on a bus station):
  - Discard a Clue card → move to any location in that card’s town, **or**
  - Discard a Clue card matching your **current** town → move to any location on the board.
- **Use a Gate** — move from one **open** gate to another open gate; roll Sanity die after moving. Cannot move to or from **sealed** gates. If you end on a Shoggoth, roll Sanity die **twice** (gate + Shoggoth).

**Other:**

- **Defeat a Cultist** — remove **1** cultist from your location → supply.
- **Defeat a Shoggoth** — **3** actions on the **same** turn; remove **1** Shoggoth → supply; draw **1** Relic from the Relic pile (other removal effects do **not** grant Relics).
- **Give or Take 1 Relic** — same location; both agree.
- **Give or Take 1 Clue** — give or take a Clue matching your **current** town; same location; both agree; receiver over **7** cards must immediately discard or play a Relic.
- **Seal a Gate** — on a gate, discard **5** Clue cards matching that gate’s town; place the matching **Seal** token; remove **1** cultist from **each** location in that town → supply.

#### Draw 2 Player cards

- If fewer than **2** cards remain before drawing → team loses (do not reshuffle).
- Resolve **Evil Stirs** completely before playing a Relic; then enforce **hand limit 7**.
- Do **not** draw replacement cards; remove Evil Stirs from the game.

**Evil Stirs** — resolve in order:

- **Fight the madness** — roll the Sanity die.
- **Awakening** — reveal the next Old One (apply its effect).
- **A Shoggoth appears** — draw the **bottom** Summoning card; place **1** Shoggoth on that location; discard the card to the Summoning discard pile. Lose if no Shoggoths remain in the supply.
- **Cultists regroup** — shuffle the Summoning **discard pile only**; place it on top of the Summoning deck.

If **2** Evil Stirs are drawn together, resolve all four steps twice (Relics may be played between the two resolutions).

#### Summoning

- **Summoning rate** — number under the rightmost revealed Old One, or **2** if none are revealed yet. Use the rate at the **start** of this step (changes mid-step apply next turn).
- Flip that many Summoning cards, one at a time:
  - Place **1** cultist on the named location → discard the card.
  - Location already has **3** cultists → **awakening ritual** instead of placing a 4th.
  - Card shows Shoggoth-move icon → move each Shoggoth **1** location toward the nearest **open** gate (active player breaks ties). Shoggoth on an open gate that must move → remove Shoggoth to supply and trigger **awakening ritual**.
- If the Summoning deck is empty, shuffle the discard to form a new deck (cards revealed **this** step stay in the discard).

## **Additional details**

---

### Sanity die

**Roll when:**

- After completing **Use a Gate**
- After resolving a **Relic** card’s effect
- Entering a location with a Shoggoth, or a Shoggoth enters your location
- First step of an **Evil Stirs** card

| Result | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Blank** | No effect |
| **Delusional** | Lose **1** sanity token |
| **Psychotic** | Lose **2** sanity tokens |
| **Paranoid** | Add **2** cultists to your location |

### Going insane

- At **0** sanity tokens, flip Investigator card to **insane** side (**−1** action, insane ability only).
- Cannot lose further sanity while insane.
- **All** players insane → lose.
- **Seal a Gate** while insane → choose **Church** or **Hospital**; flip sane; restore full starting sanity, actions, and abilities; gain **+1** action this turn (mandatory — cannot stay insane).

### Awakening ritual

- Reveal the next Old One and apply its effect.
- If **Cthulhu** awakens → immediate loss.
- No outbreaks or chain outbreaks in this game.

### Sealed gates

- No new cultists or Shoggoths may be placed in that gate’s town.
- Flip the Seal token to the **Elder Sign** side as a reminder.

### Relic cards

- Playing a Relic is **not** an action.
- Any player may play a Relic on any turn (if **Yog-Sothoth** is awake, **active player only**).
- Cannot play between drawing and resolving a card.
- After resolving, remove the Relic from the game; the player rolls the Sanity die.

### Win and lose

**Win** — all **4** gates sealed (immediate).

**Lose** — any of:

- **Cthulhu** awakens
- Cannot place a cultist when required
- Cannot place a Shoggoth when required
- Cannot draw **2** Player cards after the Actions step
- All players are insane

### Role abilities (quick reference)

| Role | Sane | Insane |
|------|------|--------|
| **Detective** | Seal a Gate with **4** matching Clues (not 5). | Same seal cost; **Give/Take Clue** costs **2** actions for you or the other player involved. |
| **Doctor** | Up to **5** actions per turn. | Up to **4** actions per turn. |
| **Driver** | **Walk:** may move up to **2** locations; ignore **Ithaqua**. | **Walk:** **must** move **2** locations; ignore **Ithaqua**. |
| **Hunter** | **Defeat Cultist** removes **all** cultists; once per turn **Defeat Shoggoth** for **1** action. | First entry each turn into a cultist-free location: roll Sanity die — on Delusional/Psychotic, place **1** cultist instead of losing sanity; Blank/Paranoid ignored. Still removes all cultists when defeating. |
| **Magician** | Hand limit **8**; start with **1** Relic; **Give/Take Relic** is free anytime (even off-turn) with a player in your location. | Hand limit **7**; may take Relics but not give; each turn must play ≥**1** Relic if you hold any (no Sanity roll for Relics). |
| **Occultist** | **1** action: move **1** cultist up to **2** locations; **2** actions: move **1** Shoggoth **1** location. | **1** action: move up to **2** cultists **1** location each. |
| **Reporter** | **Take Bus:** discard **any** Clue → any location; **1** action: move between any two bus stations. | Cannot **Take Bus**; once per turn (free): take **1** Clue matching your town from the Player discard pile. |

### Old One effects (when revealed)

| Old One | Type | Effect |
|---------|------|--------|
| **Atlach-Nacha** | Instant | Each investigator: add **1** cultist to your location **or** lose **1** sanity (insane or at 1 sanity must add cultist). |
| **Azathoth** | Permanent | Remove **3** cultists from the game permanently; lose if ≤**2** cultists remain in supply when revealed. |
| **Hastur** | — | Draw bottom Summoning card; place **1** Shoggoth; discard card; move each Shoggoth **1** step toward nearest open gate. |
| **Ithaqua** | Permanent | To **Walk** out of a location with **2+** cultists, first **Defeat a Cultist** there (other movement exempt; lifted if cultists reduced to **1** by other means). |
| **Shudde M’ell** | Instant | Team loses **3** / **4** / **5** total sanity (2 / 3 / 4 players). |
| **Tsathoggua** | Instant | Team discards **2** / **3** / **4** total Player cards (2 / 3 / 4 players); discard all you can if short. |
| **Yig** | Permanent | **Seal a Gate** also requires **1** Clue from a **connected** town (Detective discount does not apply; **Mi-go Eye** Relic can). |
| **Yog-Sothoth** | Permanent | Only the **active player** may play Relic cards. |

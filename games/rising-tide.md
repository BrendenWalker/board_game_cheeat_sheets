# Pandemic: Rising Tide

Cooperative — 2–5 players. Build all 4 hydraulic structures before the floods win.

## **Setup**

### Board setup

- Place board; set **5 ports**, **5 pumping stations**, and **36 water cubes** nearby.
- Place **4 hydraulic structure tokens** on the board (as shown).
- Place **1** dike on each red dike location (**2** locations start with **2** dikes).
- Return **Objective cards**, **population cubes**, and **Population Loss** card to the box (variant only).
- **Sea level and starting water:**
  - Sea Level marker on the lowest **2** space.
  - **2** water cubes in **Noordzee** and **Zuiderzee** (seas).
  - **Wieringermeer:** 1 cube.
  - **Markerwaard:** 2 cubes.
  - **Flevoland:** 2 cubes.
  - **Noordoostpolder:** 2 cubes.
- **Degrade regions (Dike Failure deck):**
  - Shuffle Dike Failure cards.
  - Flip **3** cards → degrade each matching region **3** times.
  - Flip **3** more → degrade each region **1** time.
  - Flip **3** more → degrade each region **1** time.
  - Place these **9** cards faceup on the Dike Failure discard pile; remainder forms the deck.
- **Player deck:**
  - Set aside Storm cards until player setup is done.
  - Choose difficulty — Introductory **6** / Standard **7** / Heroic **8** Storm cards; remove unused Storm cards.
  - Shuffle Event cards; randomly set aside the count for your player number (see Player setup).
  - Add those Events to the Region cards; return remaining Events to the box unseen.
  - Shuffle Player deck (Regions + Events).
  - Divide into facedown piles equal to the number of Storm cards used.
  - Shuffle **1** Storm card into each pile.
  - Stack piles to form the draw deck (smaller piles on the bottom).
  - Place **Reminder** token next to the Player deck (move onto the deck after your first pumping station is built).
- **Initial water flow** (before the first turn):
  - For each region with **3** water cubes, add cubes to each adjacent **low-elevation** region not blocked by a dike until each has at least **2** (skip regions that already have 2–3).
  - For each region with **2** water cubes, add cubes to each adjacent low-elevation region not blocked by a dike until each has at least **1** (skip regions that already have 1+).
  - Water never flows across dikes or into **high-elevation** regions.

**Degrade a region:** remove **1** dike (choose if several); if none remain, add **1** water cube (max **3** in a region; during setup only, excess placements have no effect). Dikes on a border belong to both regions.

### Player setup

- Each player takes a **Reference** card.
- Shuffle Role cards; deal **1** faceup to each player with its pawn.
- Place all pawns in **Delfland**; return unused Roles, pawns, and Reference cards to the box.
- **Events in deck and starting hands** (faceup):

| Players | Events in deck | Initial hand |
|---------|----------------|--------------|
| 2 | 4 | 4 |
| 3 | 5 | 3 |
| 4 | 6 | 2 |
| 5 | 8 | 2 |

- Keep all Player cards faceup on the table.

### Choosing starting player

Player whose Region card lists the shortest **Primary Defense Line** goes first.

## **Play**

### Step-by-step turn

1. Take up to **4 actions** (any order, any combination).
2. **Operate pumps.**
3. Draw **2 Player cards** (resolve Storms).
4. **Dikes fail.**
5. **Water flows.**

Play passes left. Discuss freely; the active player makes final decisions.

### Commonly overlooked rules

- Cannot **Build Dike** while your region contains water cubes (some Events/structures override this).
- When sea level rises **2→3** or **3→4**, add **1** water cube to **each sea** so cube count matches the new sea level.
- Cannot remove water cubes from **Noordzee** or **Zuiderzee** (unless **Afsluitdijk** is built — then Zuiderzee is a region).
- **Return to Port** — you need not be in a port; move to any region that has one.
- No replacement draw after drawing a **Storm** card.
- On **your** turn, you may **take** a matching Region card from another player if you share that region (both must agree).
- **Hand limit 7** applies at all times, including immediately after receiving a card.
- **Delfland** and **Flevoland** are not adjacent; **Markerwaard** and **Kromme Rijn** are not adjacent.

### Turn step details

#### Actions (4 per turn)

**Movement** (each costs 1 action):

- **Drive/Ferry** — move to an adjacent low- or high-elevation region.
- **Sail** — discard a Region card → move to that region.
- **Charter Boat** — discard a Region card matching your **current** region → move anywhere.
- **Return to Port** — move to any region containing a port.

**Other** (each costs 1 action unless noted):

- **Pump** — remove **1** water cube from your region → supply.
- **Build Dike** — place a dike bordering your region (your region must have **no** water cubes); unlimited dikes per location; if supply empty, move a dike from any region instead.
- **Build Pumping Station** — discard a Region card matching your current region; **1** per region; relocate from another region if all are placed.
- **Build Port** — discard a Region card matching your current region; **1** per region; relocate from another region if all are placed.
- **Share Resources** — give or take a Region card matching your current region with another player in the same region (both agree); receiver over **7** cards must immediately discard or play an Event.
- **Build Hydraulic Structure** — at a structure site, discard **5** Region cards of that color → place the token. **Afsluitdijk:** build from **Wieringermeer** or **Fryslân** (site spans both).

#### Operate pumps

- For each pumping station in a region with at least **1** water cube, remove **1** water cube from **1** low-elevation region reachable through a path of regions (each in the path must contain a water cube); path cannot cross **seas** or **dikes**; cube → supply.
- Operate multiple stations in any order.

#### Draw 2 Player cards

- If fewer than **2** cards remain before drawing → team loses (do not reshuffle).
- Resolve any **Storm** cards immediately; then enforce **hand limit 7** (discard or play Events).
- Do **not** draw replacement cards after a Storm; return Storm cards to the box.

**Storm** — resolve in order:

- **Sea Levels Rise** — advance Sea Level marker **1** space; if sea level hits **3** or **4**, add cubes to **Noordzee** and **Zuiderzee** until each matches the new sea level.
- **Major Breach** — draw bottom Dike Failure card; degrade that region **3** times; if it already has **3** water cubes and no dikes before finishing, trigger **1** flood there instead of placing a 4th cube; discard the card. Lose if the supply cannot provide cubes needed.
- **When It Rains, It Pours** — shuffle the Dike Failure discard pile (including the card just drawn) on top of the Dike Failure deck.

If **2** Storms are drawn together, resolve all three steps twice; Events may be played between the two resolutions.

#### Dikes fail

- Flip Dike Failure cards equal to the **current sea level** (one at a time).
- For each card, degrade that region **1** time → discard the card.
- If a region has **no** dikes and already has **3** water cubes, trigger a **flood** instead of placing a 4th cube.

#### Water flows

- If either sea has **4** water cubes, add cubes to each adjacent region until each has at least **3**.
- For each region with **3** water cubes, add cubes to each adjacent region (not blocked by dike, not high-elevation) until each has at least **2**.
- For each region with **2** water cubes, add cubes to each adjacent region until each has at least **1**.

## **Additional details**

### Floods

- Place **1** water cube in each adjacent **low-elevation** region not separated by a dike bordering **both** regions.
- Adjacent region already has **3** cubes → **chain-reaction flood** there after the current region resolves.
- During a chain reaction, do not add cubes to regions that already flooded (or chain-flooded) from this Dike Failure card.
- **Seas** are not regions and cannot flood; only seas may hold **4** water cubes.

### Hydraulic structures

| Structure | Build from | Benefit |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **Afsluitdijk** (purple) | Wieringermeer or Fryslân | Zuiderzee becomes a low-elevation region (no longer rises with sea level; max 3 cubes; can enter, pump, build port/pump); functions as a permanent dike between Zuiderzee and Noordzee; cannot be removed by Dike Failure |
| **Deltawerken** (orange) | Schouwen-Duiveland | Place a dike adjacent to Noordzee in Voorne-Putten, Goeree-Overflakkee, Schouwen-Duiveland, Walcheren, and Zeeuws-Vlaanderen |
| **Normaliseringswerken** (yellow) | Rijn en IJssel | Place up to **4** dikes on yellow region(s) |
| **Ruimte Voor De Rivier** (green) | Roer en Overmaas | Remove up to **6** water cubes from green regions |

### Win and lose

**Win** — all **4** hydraulic structures built (immediate).

**Lose** — any of:

- Cannot place water cubes actually needed on the board
- Cannot draw **2** Player cards after the Actions and Operate Pumps steps

### Event cards

- Playing an Event is **not** an action; any player may play on any turn.
- Cannot play an Event between drawing and resolving a card.
- Only Player cards count toward hand limit.

### Role abilities (quick reference)

| Role | Ability |
|------|---------|
| **Carpenter** | As an action, build a pumping station in your region (no card). May Build Dike in a region with water cubes. |
| **Pump Operator** | Pump: remove **1** cube from an adjacent region instead of yours; then you may remove **1** more from your region. |
| **Director** | As an action, move any pawn (with permission) to a region with at least **1** water cube. |
| **Sanitation Engineer** | As an action, take a Region card matching your region from the Player discard pile. |
| **Hydraulic Engineer** | Build Dike: place **1** additional dike in that location (your region must still have no water cubes). |
| **Warehouse Manager** | As an action while in a region with a port, give any Region card to any player. |
| **Port Master** | As an action, build a port in your region (no card). As an action while in a region with a port, move to any region. |

### Variable objectives (variant)

- Use Objective cards, population cubes, and Population Loss card from the box instead of the standard win condition.
- See rulebook for objective setup, **Expand Population** action, and population-loss loss condition.

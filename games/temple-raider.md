# Temple Raider

Racing — 2–4 players. First to get all 10 relics off the temple wins.

## **Setup**

### Board setup

- Place temple in the center.
- Connect each player board to the temple.
- **Dice in temple center:**
  - **2 players:** 3 white dice
  - **3 players:** white dice + yellow die
  - **4 players:** white dice + yellow + red dice

### Player setup

- Each player takes a player board, **10 relics** (#1–#10), and a personal **16-card** power deck.
- Place all 10 relics on the **top starting row** on your side of the temple.
- Shuffle your power deck; place it beside your board.
- Draw **3** cards → place on the three power card slots at the bottom of your board.

### Choosing starting player

- Roll a die — highest goes first; play passes clockwise.
- **Setup handicaps** (after first player is chosen):
  - **2nd player:** move **#2** relic down 1 step
  - **3rd player:** move **#4** relic down 1 step
  - **4th player:** move **#6** relic down 1 step

## **Play**

### Step-by-step turn

1. **Active player** rolls all dice in play.
2. **Active player** chooses **2** dice → move relics and/or activate power cards.
3. **Other players** draft remaining dice clockwise (**left of roller first**), **1 die** each → move or activate a card.
4. Turn ends when all dice are drafted; next player clockwise rolls.

### Commonly overlooked rules

- Only the **active player** may combine two dice (wild 11/12, combined moves/cards). Drafters use **1 die** only.
- **Wild** and **eruption** checks apply when the active player’s **two** chosen dice are used together.
- **Resupply** is available **at any time** — including when it is not your turn — if you cannot or choose not to move any relics or use any power cards.
- **Storage full** + wild → also move your **smallest** relic (lowest # still on the temple) down 1 step.
- Before choosing dice, you may move one relic **storage → camp** to add **+1** to one die you will use this roll.

### Turn step details

#### Active player — 2 dice

**Move relics** — on your side of the temple only:

- Use each die separately → move the relic matching that die’s value down **1 step**.
- Combine both dice → move the relic matching the **total** down 1 step.
- Example: 4 and 5 → move **#4** and **#5**, or move **#9**.

**Activate power cards** — die value must match the card’s slot number:

- 2 moves, 2 cards, 1 move + 1 card, combined move, or combined card activation.
- Perform the action printed on the card.
- Discard activated card(s); draw to refill the **3** display slots.

#### Draft dice — 1 die each

- Clockwise from the player left of the roller until all dice are taken.
- Use the die to move one matching relic **or** activate one matching power card.
- Resupply applies instead of using the die if you cannot or choose not to (see below).

#### Wilds and eruptions

**Wild** — active player only; combine two chosen dice to **11** or **12**:

- Move **any one** of your relics down 1 step.
- Cannot activate power cards with a wild.
- If storage is **full**, also move your **smallest** relic on the temple down 1 step.

**Eruption** — active player only; both chosen dice show **lava icons**:

- Move **1** relic on a **lava space** up 1 step (any player’s relic).
- **Double eruption** (power card) → 2 relics; **triple eruption** → 3 relics.
- Cannot move a relic up if another relic is **directly behind** it on that column.

#### Resupply

At any time you cannot or choose not to move **any** relics or use **any** power cards — **even when it is not your turn**:

- Move one relic **camp → storage** (see Escaping relics), **or**
- Move one of your relics **left one number** — only if that space is empty.

#### Escaping relics

- A relic that moves off the last temple step **escapes**.
- Place it on your board in **altar**, **storage**, or **camp**.

## **Additional details**

### Win condition

First player with **all 10 relics** off the temple (on their player board) wins immediately.

### Player board areas

| Area | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Altar** | Escape destination; some power cards may reference relics here |
| **Storage** | Escape destination; limited slots — when **full**, wild bonus applies |
| **Camp** | Escape destination; moving **storage → camp** before rolling gives **+1** to one die |

### Power cards

- Personal deck of **16**; **3** face-up on your board at all times.
- Activate by spending a die that **matches the card’s number**; resolve text on the card.
- Some cards cause eruptions or other effects — follow the card.
- Card text is not repeated here; read cards at the table.

### Dice by player count

| Players | Dice |
|---------|------|
| 2 | 3 white |
| 3 | 3 white + 1 yellow |
| 4 | 3 white + 1 yellow + 1 red |
